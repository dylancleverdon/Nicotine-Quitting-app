# Firewatch changelog

Newest first. Each release is a "## <version>" heading; the app shows these notes in "What's new".

## 0.2.0 · 2026-09-27
- Your tier: after the baseline week, the home screen shows where you are on the fire ladder, from Wildfire down to Clear Air. There are small steps one piece at a time, so stepping down is never a big jump.
- Next-piece battery: shows when your next piece is recommended at your target pace, or that you're clear for one. It never nags, and it pauses while you sleep.
- Step down when you're ready: after holding your rung, Firewatch offers the next one down. It uses your own cravings to judge whether you're ready, and offers a step back up if a rung is too tough right now.
- Friend's vape button: logs unknown doses as a range. You can save a friend's vape for one tap next time, or count puffs on the widget.
- Insights tab, full of graphs: blood-level wave, daily totals, tier staircase, heatmap, clear hours, money saved, what you can ride out, nicotine quality (gum is the gold standard), forecasts, badges, monthly recap, day barcode and more.
- Two home-screen widgets: quick log (with the battery and a puff counter) and a one-tap craving logger.
- Optional: daily check-in, morning delay goal, wind-down before bed, reward to save toward, watch overlay (heart rate and sleep from Health Connect).
- Tag what's going on during a craving to track which triggers you're beating.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-09-26
- First version of Firewatch by Baastik Labs.
- One-tap logging for Zyn 3 mg and 6 mg and nicotine gum 2 mg and 4 mg, with undo. Hold a button to set the time, amount, how long it stayed in, coffee/soda and what was going on.
- Everything counts in pieces: what one 4 mg gum delivers into your blood.
- Craving button with a 1–10 scale that describes what each level feels like. Tap "It passed" when it does.
- Good morning and Good night buttons.
- Calendar: every day coloured by how much you used. Tap a day to see, edit or add doses.
- Baseline week: for 7 days Firewatch just watches.
- Updates install themselves, with a backup before every update and a way to go back to the previous version.
- Export and import your data.
